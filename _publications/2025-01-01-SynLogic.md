SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond

This paper presents SynLogic, a novel approach for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale to improve logical reasoning capabilities in large language models. The method addresses the challenge of generating high-quality reasoning datasets that can be used to train and evaluate LLMs on logical tasks.

### Abstract

Large language models have shown remarkable capabilities in various natural language tasks, but their reasoning abilities remain limited. This paper introduces SynLogic, a framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale. Our approach generates high-quality reasoning examples with verifiable answers, enabling more effective training and evaluation of LLMs on logical reasoning tasks.

### Key Contributions

- A novel method for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale
- A dataset for training and evaluating LLMs on logical reasoning
- Experimental results showing improved reasoning capabilities
