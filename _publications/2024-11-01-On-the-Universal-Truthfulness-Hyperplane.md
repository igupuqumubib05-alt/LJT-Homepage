On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs

This paper, published at EMNLP 2024, investigates the universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models. We explore the concept of truthfulness in LLMs and identify a hyperplane that separates truthful from untruthful responses.

### Abstract

Large language models often generate untruthful responses, but the underlying mechanisms are not well understood. This paper identifies a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside LLMs that can be used to detect and mitigate untruthful responses. Our findings provide insights into improving the truthfulness of LLMs.

### Key Contributions

- Identification of a universal truthfulness hyperplane in LLMs
- Methods to detect and mitigate untruthful responses
- A GitHub code repository: Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane
