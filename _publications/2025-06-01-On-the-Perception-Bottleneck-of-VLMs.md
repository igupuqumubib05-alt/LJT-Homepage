On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding

This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for chart understanding. We analyze how VLMs perceive and interpret visual information in charts and propose methods to improve their performance.

### Abstract

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have shown promise in understanding charts and visual data, but there is a perception bottleneck that limits their effectiveness. This paper studies this bottleneck and proposes solutions to improve VLMs' chart understanding capabilities.

### Key Contributions

- Analysis of the perception bottleneck in VLMs for chart understanding
- Methods to improve VLMs' visual perception for charts
- A GitHub code repository: Vision4Chart
