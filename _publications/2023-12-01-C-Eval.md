C-Eval: A Multi-Level Multi-Discipline Chinese Evaluation Suite for Foundation Models

This paper, published at NeurIPS 2023, presents C-Eval, a multi-level multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. The evaluation suite covers multiple disciplines and levels to comprehensively evaluate Chinese foundation models.

### Abstract

There is a need for comprehensive evaluation suites for foundation models, especially for Chinese language models. This paper introduces C-Eval, a multi-level multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite that can be used to evaluate foundation models across various tasks and disciplines.

### Key Contributions

- A multi-level multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite
- Comprehensive coverage of various tasks and disciplines
- Useful for evaluating Chinese foundation models
