Composing Parameter-Efficient Modules with Arithmetic Operations

This paper, published at NeurIPS 2023, explores composing parameter-efficient modules with arithmetic operations. The method enables more efficient training and inference of large models.

### Abstract

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods have become popular for adapting large models to specific tasks. This paper proposes a novel approach for composing parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations, which enables more efficient adaptation of large models.

### Key Contributions

- A novel method for composing parameter-efficient modules
- Arithmetic operations for efficient module composition
- Experimental results showing improved efficiency
